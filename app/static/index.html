Horn Signal Studio

Rehearse railway and marine horn signals, check crossing timing and estimate how far a horn carries.

Signal

- or L long, o or S short, separated by spaces.

The preview is a soft three-note chord through your speakers, not a real horn. Keep the volume low.

Crossing onset (49 CFR 222.21)

The horn starts at least 15 and at most 20 seconds before the locomotive enters the crossing. Above 60 mph it need not start more than a quarter mile (1,320 ft) out.

Level at distance

Free-field inverse-square estimate (6 dB less per doubling). Locomotive horns must measure 96-110 dB(A) at 100 ft (49 CFR 229.129). Ground, buildings and air absorption make real levels lower.
